Project Description:
Plintron DSM portal contains various modules like Retailers, Resellers, Wholesaler, Service provider and Admin Module.
	This is a web based application to maintain the process of LYCAMOBILE Sales and management Distribution Sales Managements (DSM) application in the Plintron MVNE platform enables the MVNOs to manage the SIMs and Vouchers distribution process to the retailers without any hassle. 

	The SIMs and voucher details are uploaded on various applications in the MVNE platform. Once the upload process is completed, the SIMs and Vouchers must be distributed to the retailers. The retailer in turn can sell the stock (SIMs and vouchers) to subscribers. 

	The retailer can also do top-up for subscribers. The stock and top-up amount will be allocated to users in the DSM application and finally allocated to retailers. The stock and top-up allocation process undergoes several authentication processes to make sure that they are efficiently distributed to the retailers and subscribers.
	
	Also, the MVNO can define incentives to various levels of users in the DSM application. Based on the stock sold to the subscribers, each user-levels involved in the stock distribution process will get the respective incentive.
	In the similar way, the excess stock must be returned back to the subsequent high-level user in order to ensure that the stock is not getting stagnated at one user-level.


The Distribution process goes like below steps,
Wholesaler
Branch Office
Head Office
Sub-Wholesaler
Retailers
